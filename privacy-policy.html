Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiz App handles information when you use the Quiz App mobile application and related services.

2. Information We May Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Authentication-related information required to provide your account

Quiz and Account Activity

  • Quiz questions and answers submitted through the app
  • Whether submitted answers are correct
  • Points and related account activity
  • Quiz availability or cooldown information needed to operate the quiz system

Technical and Advertising Information

The app may process technical information and advertising-related information through third-party services used by the app, such as authentication, database, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services, where applicable.

Quiz App does not intentionally request precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera information unless a future feature specifically requires such access.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Provide quiz questions and process submitted answers
  • Calculate and display points
  • Apply quiz availability and cooldown rules
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, cheating, or unauthorized activity
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service
  • Provide advertising or other app-supported services where applicable
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Third-Party Services

Quiz App may use third-party service providers to operate parts of the application, including Firebase, Supabase, advertising providers, and other infrastructure or service providers.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and the services they provide.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the service. Passwords are not intentionally stored by Quiz App in plain text.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to provide authentication, database functionality, advertising, security, hosting, or other app functionality, or when required by law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, prevent abuse, meet security requirements, or comply with legal obligations.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the instructions on our Delete Account page.

8. Children's Privacy

Quiz App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located in countries other than your own. Where applicable, information may therefore be processed internationally.
