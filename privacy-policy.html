Quiz App Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiz App collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when you use the Quiz App mobile application.

Quiz App is designed to provide educational quizzes, record quiz results, maintain points associated with your account, and provide related app functionality.

By using Quiz App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your Firebase authentication identifier (UID).
  • Authentication-related account information required to operate your account.

Quiz and account activity

When you use Quiz App, we may process information such as:

  • Quiz questions and answers submitted through the app.
  • Whether submitted answers are correct.
  • Points earned through quizzes.
  • Quiz availability or cooldown information needed to operate the quiz system.

Technical and advertising information

Quiz App may use third-party services for authentication, cloud data processing, analytics, notifications, advertising, security, and app operation. Those services may process technical information such as device information, app activity, IP address, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, or similar technical data according to their own policies.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera content unless a future feature explicitly requires it and the appropriate permission and disclosure are provided.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and authenticate your Quiz App account.
  • Provide access to quizzes.
  • Check and record quiz answers.
  • Calculate and display points.
  • Prevent abuse, cheating, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Maintain quiz availability and cooldown controls.
  • Provide notifications or other app functionality where enabled.
  • Display and measure advertising where advertising services are used.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Quiz App uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate its features. These providers may process information on our behalf.

  • Firebase: used for user authentication and related account services.
  • Supabase: used for application database and server-side functionality.
  • Advertising providers: used to display advertisements when enabled in the app.
  • Other infrastructure providers: may be used for security, hosting, notifications, diagnostics, or other necessary app services.

Third-party providers process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Passwords and Security

Authentication is handled through the authentication service used by Quiz App. We do not intentionally store your password in plain text.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that are necessary to operate Quiz App, such as authentication, database, hosting, security, notification, and advertising providers.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, enforce our terms, protect users, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Advertising

Quiz App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. Depending on the advertising service and your device settings, those providers may use certain device or advertising information to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements.

Advertising practices are also governed by the applicable third-party advertising provider's policies and the choices available on your device.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and quiz information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

When a valid account deletion request is completed, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account, subject to any information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Quiz App account and associated personal data through our dedicated account deletion page:

Request Account Deletion

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed as described on the deletion page. Deleting an account is different from simply uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Quiz App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. International Processing

Your information may be processed or stored by service providers in countries other than your own. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal obligations, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Quiz App support/privacy contact shown on the official app website or Google Play listing.
