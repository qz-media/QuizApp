Delete Account

Request deletion of your Quiz App account and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Quiz App account, send an email to:

qz.mediaofficial@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your email so that we can identify your account and process the request:

  • Your full name
  • Your registered mobile number
  • Your referral code, if you have one
  • Your account email, if applicable
  • A clear explanation that you want to delete your Quiz App account

Please send the request from an email address that you can access. Do not send your password, OTP, Firebase credentials, payment-card details, or other sensitive authentication information.

Example:
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Name: Your Name
Mobile Number: 03XXXXXXXXX
Referral Code: YOURCODE
Request: I want to permanently delete my Quiz App account and associated personal data.

What Happens After Your Request?

We will review the request and verify that it relates to the account owner before processing deletion.

Where deletion is applicable, the account and associated personal data will be deleted, subject to information that may legitimately need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. After deletion, access to the account and associated account data may not be recoverable.

Important: Do not include your password or one-time verification codes in the deletion email.
