What happens when you request deletion?

After we verify the request, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your Quiz App account, subject to information that must or may be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory purposes.

Account deletion may remove access to your account, quiz history, points, and other information associated with the account. Deleting the app from your device does not by itself delete your account.
